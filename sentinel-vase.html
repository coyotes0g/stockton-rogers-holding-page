Sentinel Vase I + II

Standing watch, in wood.

The Sentinel Vase is a tall, architectural vessel built around a central oak column, its surfaces patterned with the end-grain of interlocking box joints — a hand-cut technique that turns the joinery itself into the surface. Three walnut fins rise the height of the column and past it, giving the piece its upright, standing-guard presence, all anchored on a solid cherry base. The oak column is open at the top, fitted with a removable glass insert sized for a single stem so it can hold a fresh cutting as readily as dried branches or faux florals.

Made to Hold a Moment

A single stem says as much as a bouquet — one bloom, one branch, given room to stand on its own. The Sentinel Vase was made for exactly that: its tall, spare form suited to a lone cutting, a dried sprig, or a faux stem kept year-round. The glass insert lifts out when you want dried or faux, and drops back in when you want water. Whatever it holds, it gives it a quiet sense of occasion.

Key Features

  • Central oak column with box-jointed faces, the interlocking end-grain forming its signature texture
  • Three walnut fins rising past the column for an upright, sculptural profile
  • Solid cherry base for a grounded, substantial feel
  • Removable glass insert — holds water for a fresh stem or two, lifts out for dried or faux arrangements
  • Felt-padded base to protect the surface it rests on
  • Hand-rubbed oil finish that brings out the depth and figure of the wood
