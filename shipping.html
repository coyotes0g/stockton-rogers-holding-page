Shipping & Returns

How we ship made-to-order pieces, and what to do if one arrives damaged.

Shipping

We ship in the continental United States. Shipping is free. We do not ship to Alaska, Hawaii, or outside the U.S.

Made-to-order pieces ship in the window already stated on the product page.

Returns

Each piece is made to order. We do not accept returns or exchanges except for damage in transit.

If your piece arrives damaged, contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos. We will repair or replace it.

Contact for damage: contact@stocktonandrogers.com, the Contact page, or (479) 396-3935.
